# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would reasonably
be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project
maintainer via a private channel (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for contact
routes). Maintainers will review and respond to reports and may remove,
edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
